# Places: building and room

**Goal:** record where you worked; office vs. home follows the place.

1. **Settings → Office & places → Places:** add building, optional room, and whether it is office or home.
2. On **Today**, pick the place. Without places, just pick Office or Home.
3. **Several places per day** (setting):
   - off — switching place changes the whole day (one location per day),
   - on — switching splits the running entry, so each part keeps its own place.

NFC tag per room: `wtt://switch-place?place=HQ%20%2F%203.14&source=nfc`.
