# Manage projects

**Goal:** add projects, see where your hours went, and put finished projects to rest — without losing anything.

1. Open **Overview**. Your projects float around the big stats bubble. Each bubble shows its hours and its share of the chosen period; bigger bubbles got more time.
2. **Add a project:** press and hold the **+ Pump** bubble (or tap it a few times) until it is fully inflated. It floats to the middle and asks for a name. Type it and press Enter. Escape or leaving it empty lets the air out.
3. **Open a project:** tap its bubble for the total, notes and settings.
4. **Put a project to rest:** drag its bubble onto the marked spot at the edge (_Let it sink_) and hold until the ring closes. The running project can't rest — stop or switch first.
5. **Bring it back:** tap the resting strip at the edge (or pinch/ctrl+scroll to zoom in). Drag a resting project onto _Bring it up_ and hold. **Back** zooms out again.
6. **Choose the resting place:** _Settings → Resting place for removed projects_ — **Seabed** (bubbles sink), **Sky** (they float up into the clouds) or **Attic** (they get packed onto a shelf).

**Result:** resting projects disappear from Today and Overview but keep all their hours, notes and exports. Nothing is ever deleted.
