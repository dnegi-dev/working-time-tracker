# Switch projects

**Goal:** know which project your hours went to — and take short breaks between them.

1. Create projects under **Projects** → type a name → **Add**.
2. On **Today**, your current project is the big **focus bubble** in the middle, showing its time today. Your other projects float around it (most recently used first), each tied to the middle by a line.
3. **Drag the focus bubble onto another project and hold it there** until the ring around it closes (you feel a tick on iPhone). Letting go early cancels. While tracking, the time is split at that moment — no gap.
4. **Focus round:** while the workday runs, the focus bubble slowly fills with water, gently floats and lets oxygen bubbles rise. After 25 minutes (change it under _Settings → Focus round_) it is full, turns orange and the **Break** bubble glows.
5. **Break:** drag onto the **Break** bubble at the bottom and hold. The project keeps counting — the break time stays with it. When you're ready, drag onto the next project (or the same one to continue); the switch happens then and a fresh focus round starts. Stopping the workday also counts as a break.
6. Automate it with a QR code or NFC tag per project: see [NFC & QR](automate-nfc-qr.md) (command `switch-project`).

With more than six projects, the **+N** bubble lists the rest. Project hours for the month are listed under **Projects**; tap a project for its total and notes. **Archive** hides finished projects without losing data.
