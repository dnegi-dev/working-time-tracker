# Track your day

**Goal:** record total working hours for today.

1. Open **Today**. At the top is the **workday bar**: slide its knob to the end and **hold it** until the ring around it closes (desktop: press <kbd>Space</kbd>). The deliberate gesture avoids starting or stopping by accident.
2. The bar shows net time worked today and the time **left**, and it **fills up** as you work towards your daily target. Once you pass the target it **turns orange** and shows your overtime.
3. Below the bar, the **project bubbles** fill the screen: drag the middle bubble onto another project and hold to switch, or onto **Break** for a short pause — see [Switch projects](switch-projects.md).
4. Slide and hold again to stop for breaks and at the end of the day. Project time pauses while the workday is stopped.

**Breaks:** if _Settings → Deduct legal breaks_ is on, the app deducts 30 min after 6 h and 45 min after 9 h — minus breaks you already took (gaps of 15 min or more between entries).

**Past midnight:** if you keep tracking past midnight, the entry is split at 00:00 so each day gets its own hours.

**Result:** Today and Overview show your hours; the entries are saved on the device.
