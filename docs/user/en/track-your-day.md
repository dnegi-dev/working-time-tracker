# Track your day

**Goal:** record total working hours for today.

1. Open **Today**. At the top is the **workday bar**. Press or tap its knob to **see the options**: the far end of the track and the pockets below it are labelled for a moment. Slide the knob to the right end and **hold it** until the ring around it closes (desktop: press <kbd>Space</kbd>). The deliberate gesture avoids starting or stopping by accident.
   - Slide **straight** to start a **home-office** day. A slightly diagonal swipe counts as straight.
   - Slide **all the way to the end first**, then go **straight down** into the pocket below it to start an **office** day (counts towards your office quota). Cutting the corner on a diagonal does not dip.
   - While you work, the knob rests on the right and shows a **play** icon.
2. The bar shows net time worked today and the time **left**, and it **fills up** as you work towards your daily target. Once you pass the target it **turns orange** and shows your overtime.
3. Below the bar, the **project bubbles** fill the screen: drag the middle bubble onto another project and hold to switch, or onto **Break** for a short pause — see [Switch projects](switch-projects.md).
4. **Lunch:** swipe the knob **straight down** from the right and hold. The workday pauses and the lunch counts towards no project. Swipe it back **up** to continue with the same project and place.
5. **End of day:** slide the knob back to the **left** end and hold. Once at the left end, go **straight down** instead to stop and **add the missing legal break** (shown as +30 or +45): your end time moves later by the break, so your worked time stays as it is.

**Breaks:** if _Settings → Deduct legal breaks_ is on, the app deducts 30 min after 6 h and 45 min after 9 h — minus breaks you already took (gaps of 15 min or more between entries). While you work, the bar shows the time you tracked (the same as the project bubble); the missing break is deducted once you stop the day.

**Holidays:** a public holiday shows under the date on Today once it is 3 days away or less.

**Past midnight:** if you keep tracking past midnight, the entry is split at 00:00 so each day gets its own hours.

**Result:** Today and Overview show your hours; the entries are saved on the device.
