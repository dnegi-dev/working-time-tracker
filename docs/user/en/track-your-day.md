# Track your day

**Goal:** record total working hours for today.

1. Open **Today** and tap **Start** (desktop: press <kbd>Space</kbd>).
2. The big number shows net time worked today; below it, time **left** (or overtime).
3. Tap **Stop** for breaks and at the end of the day. Tap **Start** again after a break.

Forgot to clock in? **+ Add entry** → enter from/to. Tap any entry to change times or project, or delete it.

**Breaks:** if _Settings → Deduct legal breaks_ is on, the app deducts 30 min after 6 h and 45 min after 9 h — minus breaks you already took (gaps of 15 min or more between entries).

**Result:** Today and Overview show your hours; the entries are saved on the device.
