# Track your day

**Goal:** record total working hours for today.

1. Open **Today**. On the left, **slide the workday knob to the end and hold it** until the ring around it closes (desktop: press <kbd>Space</kbd>). The deliberate gesture avoids starting or stopping by accident.
2. The left card shows net time worked today and time **left** (or overtime).
3. On the right is a small wheel of projects: your current project in the middle with its time today, the next one above and the one you worked on before below. **Drag the wheel** down to bring the upper project into the middle, or up for the lower one — when the header says _Release to switch_, let go (on iPhone you also feel a tick). Tapping the upper or lower project works too. Use the **⌄** button on the upper project to choose which project sits there. Switching never creates a break.
4. Slide and hold again to stop for breaks and at the end of the day. Project time pauses while the workday is stopped.

Forgot to clock in? **+ Add entry** → enter from/to. Tap any entry to change times or project, or delete it.

**Breaks:** if _Settings → Deduct legal breaks_ is on, the app deducts 30 min after 6 h and 45 min after 9 h — minus breaks you already took (gaps of 15 min or more between entries).

**Past midnight:** if you keep tracking past midnight, the entry is split at 00:00 so each day gets its own hours.

**Result:** Today and Overview show your hours; the entries are saved on the device.
