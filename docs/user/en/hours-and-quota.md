# Hours left & office quota

**Goal:** know how much you still need to work and how often you must go to the office.

1. **Settings → Working time:** set hours per weekday (e.g. 8 Mon–Fri, 0 Sat/Sun).
2. **Settings → Office & places:** choose the rule
   - **Office days per week** (e.g. 2) — the app computes the monthly/yearly target from the _actual_ workdays, or
   - **Max. home office %** (e.g. 60).
3. Pick your **region** (e.g. North Rhine-Westphalia). Public holidays come from OpenHolidays; you can paste your own iCal URL instead (`{year}` and `{region}` are replaced).
4. Open **Overview**.

**Result:**

- The big bubble in the middle shows worked hours, target and hours left, plus office days “4 of 9”. It fills up as the day goes by.
- **Tap it** to switch the period: week → month → year → all time. The dots show which one you're on.
- Your projects float around it with their hours for that period (see [Manage projects](manage-projects.md)).
- The overview fills the whole screen. On a smaller window the bubbles move closer together.

Workdays = days with a target, minus holidays and days marked vacation/sick. Required office days are rounded up. A day counts as an office day as soon as one entry is in office mode.
